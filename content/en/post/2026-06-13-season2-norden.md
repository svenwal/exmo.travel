While I visit a client on site, Saskia heads for the old town and sets off exploring on her own.

## Vejers Strand

Sometimes these things just take a while. Somehow, in Morocco, we genuinely managed not to take a single proper beach photo with Mammothon. We make up for that now in Vejers Strand.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-season2-norden/vejers-strand-01.jpeg"
  alt="Mammothon on the beach at Vejers"
  caption="The beach photo, finally"
>}}

Afterwards we stay a few days on a wonderfully quiet campsite right in the dunes. Our pitch is at the very top of the dune - reachable only with four-wheel drive.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-season2-norden/vejers-strand-02.jpeg"
