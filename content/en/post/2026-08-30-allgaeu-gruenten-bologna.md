## Allgäu and 100,000 kilometres

We have reached the beautiful home region of our daughter: we now stay in the [Allgäu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allg%C3%A4u) for two weeks. That ends the big northern loop and with it the first half of Season 2.

During this time Sven drives Mammothon over to the [Dachstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dachstein_Mountains). A repair is due there that unfortunately found no room in the calendar in spring. And on the way there is one more special milestone: Mammothon passes 100,000 kilometres.

  src="/images/blog/2026-08-allgaeu-gruenten-bologna/kilometerzaehler-100000.jpeg"

From the Dachstein Sven continues straight on to [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), and from there he flies to [Scandinavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandinavia) for a few appointments. Planning is everything.

## The Grünten

At the weekend the [Grünten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gr%C3%BCnten) is finally on the programme. A few years ago we had already almost made it to the top. It was Easter then, and the last 100 metres of altitude consisted of nothing but ice and snow. This time, in August, the problem is more the opposite: it is almost too warm.

  src="/images/blog/2026-08-allgaeu-gruenten-bologna/gruenten-aufstieg-wiese.jpeg"

## Dachstein

The following weekend we take a rental car, drive over to the [Dachstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dachstein_Mountains) and finally get our home back.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-08-allgaeu-gruenten-bologna/dachstein-lkw-hof.jpeg"
  alt="Several expedition vehicles parked in a yard in front of wooded slopes"
  caption="Expedition vehicles in the yard"
>}}

Then it is quickly back to [Salzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salzburg) to return the rental car, and the second half of this season starts.

## Bologna and the Apennines

We head south on the [Tauern Autobahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tauern_Autobahn) and, after a short stop in [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria), continue across the [Po Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Po_Valley) in northern Italy all the way to [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna). There, of course, comes the first Italian meal first of all. Pasta, as it should be.

With well-filled stomachs we then drive into the mountains south of Bologna, into the [Apennines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apennine_Mountains), and venture a tour up the mountain. Because of the heat, however, we do not make progress quite as quickly as hoped.
