## Arribes del Duero

Back to nature again: we head a little further west into the [Arribes del Duero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arribes_del_Duero) nature park.

The weather is still not entirely on our side, but right on cue at the end of the working day a rain-free window of two hours opens up. Perfect for a short walk to the gorge.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-arribes-sierra/mieza-01.jpeg"
  alt="Gorge in the Arribes del Duero nature park"
  caption="The gorge of the Arribes"
>}}

The landscape is deeply impressive - and griffon vultures circle above us everywhere. Nature's own cinema.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-arribes-sierra/mieza-02.jpeg"
  alt="Griffon vultures over the Arribes del Duero"
  caption="Griffon vultures as far as you can see"
>}}

The village of Mieza feels completely deserted. Out of season there really seems to be almost nobody here: windows and doors are boarded up, waiting for spring.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-arribes-sierra/mieza-04.jpeg"
  alt="The seemingly abandoned village of Mieza"
  caption="Mieza waiting for spring"
>}}

## Reversing through the village

We drive towards the mountains in pouring rain. On arrival the car park turns out to be very steeply sloped - so we go back two villages. Partly in reverse through a narrow village, with a lorry coming the other way.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-arribes-sierra/navarredonda-01.jpeg"
  alt="Narrow village street in the Sierra de Francia"
  caption="Not every street is meant for us"
>}}

In Navarredonda de la Rinconada my current night shift creates the chance to hike during the day. And for that the weather finally shows some mercy: blue sky and sunshine.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-arribes-sierra/navarredonda-02.jpeg"
  alt="View from the hike to the Pico Cervero"
  caption="Blue sky at last"
>}}

We hike up to the Pico Cervero at 1,465 metres, in places against a still fairly strong wind - with a superb view.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-arribes-sierra/navarredonda-04.jpeg"
  alt="View from the Pico Cervero"
  caption="Pico Cervero, 1,465 metres"
>}}

## La Alberca

With the weather still wet and gloomy we look for a pitch with an electricity hookup again. That takes us deeper into the Sierra de Francia, to [La Alberca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Alberca).

And where exactly have we ended up? A beautiful old village that is evidently a very popular holiday destination among Spaniards. The historic centre has a charm all of its own.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-arribes-sierra/la-alberca-01.jpeg"

There are excellent walking routes all around, and we take on three of them. On Friday Saskia walks the big northern loop - in the rain. On Saturday we head south together, and this time the weather plays along.
