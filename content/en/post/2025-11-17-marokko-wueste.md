## 170 kilos per wheel

We carry on through the sand with a view of the big dunes. You barely know where to point the camera first.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-wueste/radwechsel-01.jpeg"
  alt="View of the big dunes in Morocco"
  caption="A view of the big dunes"
>}}

Today wheel change training is on the schedule. We had already done it involuntarily - but now everything is worked through calmly and step by step. Exactly as it should be.

For context: one wheel weighs around 170 kilos, and the axles carry 6 tonnes at the front and 10 tonnes at the rear. You develop a certain respect for the engineering - and for what a vehicle like this puts up with.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-wueste/radwechsel-03.jpeg"
  alt="Wheel change training in the desert"
  caption="This time without the hurry"
>}}

After the work is done we have more than earned a really good dinner.

## The Tajine mountain

What a place to wake up. We are parked among dunes, the sun is slowly rising, and in front of us sits the "Tajine mountain" - which genuinely looks like a [tajine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajine).

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-wueste/et-taous-01.jpeg"
  alt="Sunrise among the dunes near Et-Taous"
  caption="This is how you wake up here"
>}}

After breakfast comes the next lesson. Practising very tight turns, we end up ploughing the ground so thoroughly that nobody really wants to drive there afterwards.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-wueste/et-taous-02.jpeg"
  alt="The Tajine mountain in the desert near Et-Taous"
  caption="The Tajine mountain"
>}}

For once we stay in the same place today. That means plenty of time for photographs - and at night I try for the first time to capture the astonishing Milky Way with the camera.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-wueste/et-taous-04.jpeg"
  alt="Dune landscape near Et-Taous"
  caption="Et-Taous"
>}}

## The day of sand

We wake at the dunes and watch the sunrise from there. Then it is on through the sand to the next stopping place - which is, naturally, also sandy.

  alt="Sunrise at the dunes near Sidi Ali"
  caption="Sunrise at the dunes"

Here we practise reversing into a space. That particular skill, though, the last few months of daily life had already taught me fairly thoroughly.

  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-wueste/sidi-ali-02.jpeg"

## Technical test day

Today every spare wheel mount in the group gets tested under real conditions: sand, dirt, awkward angles. Not every design copes well, and refitting the wheels in particular turns out to be a genuine challenge for some.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-wueste/h-ssyia-01.jpeg"
  alt="Working on the vehicles in the desert"
  caption="Technical test day"
>}}

We had already worked through that topic intensively right at the start with our flat. Instead we spend today reworking how the motorbikes are lashed down - the terrain of the last few days has taken its toll.

  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-wueste/h-ssyia-03.jpeg"
