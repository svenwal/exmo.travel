## Strynfjellet

Onwards through the Norwegian mountains. We spend the last night on another free Nortrip pitch. In return we restock our supplies with produce straight from the farm. It is exactly this combination of special places and local producers that appeals to us.

## Trollstigen

Two Norwegian highlights in a single day. One place we had always skipped on our previous trips to Norway is the [Trollstigen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trollstigen). This time it is finally happening.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-trollstigen/trollstigen-01.jpeg"
  alt="The hairpins of the Trollstigen"
  caption="Trollstigen"
>}}

First, though, the route takes us to [Geiranger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geiranger). The drive there is demanding: countless tight hairpins, steep sections and, on top of that, plenty of coaches ferrying cruise passengers to the viewpoints. In Mammothon you stay focused.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-trollstigen/trollstigen-02.jpeg"
  alt="View of the Geirangerfjord"
  caption="Geiranger"
>}}

Having driven the hairpins down into Geiranger and back up the other side, the Trollstigen itself finally follows.

  alt="Mountain road in Norway"
  caption="And then, at last"
