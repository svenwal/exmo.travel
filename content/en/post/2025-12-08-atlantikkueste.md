## Separate ways

Today our group goes separate ways. Some take the fastest route to Tanger Med for the ferry, others continue their journey further south through Morocco. And us? We take an entirely different course - west, to the coast.

That means first of all: straight across Marrakesh. This time genuinely off the tourist routes, and thanks to roadworks, criss-cross through the city as well.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-atlantikkueste/aquermoud-01.jpeg"

In the end we reach a small campsite right on the beach near Aquermoud. Here it is time to breathe out and recover: a good restaurant, good showers, and the sea almost entirely to ourselves.

## Inland

After a few restful days on the beach we want a bit more green and a bit less sound of surf. So we drive a few kilometres inland - Saskia on the motorbike, me in Mammothon.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-atlantikkueste/ounagha-01.jpeg"
  alt="Inland landscape near Ounagha"
  caption="A bit more green"

Our new pitch is completely different again: surrounded by trees, gravelled bays, a touch larger - and even with a mini golf course.

{{< figure

And from here we also set off on a short trip with the motorbikes to [Essaouira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essaouira). This time we take a little more time over it.

## Into the medina after all

We head back to Marrakesh - once more right across the city and, thanks to roadworks, through streets rather less intended for large trucks.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-atlantikkueste/marrakesch-01.jpeg"
  alt="Streets in Marrakesh"
  caption="Once more across the city"
>}}

Again we try to park near the medina. Again there is a big event on and the car parks are unavailable. So, straight to the campsite.

There we organise a taxi that has no problem with dogs. We had been told this was impossible - it turned out to be no trouble at all. And so we get to walk the medina properly after all.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-atlantikkueste/marrakesch-02.jpeg"
  alt="Alleyways in the medina of Marrakesh"
  caption="In the medina"
>}}

And there is plenty going on: people bustling through every street, shops and cafés everywhere, and an atmosphere you do not forget in a hurry.
