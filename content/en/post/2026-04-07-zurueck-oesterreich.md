## The Lech valley

On we go, back to Austria. I picked out a campsite in the Lech valley - and once again did not look too carefully at the route there.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-zurueck-oesterreich/lechtal-01.jpeg"
  alt="Snow-covered mountain road in the Lech valley"
  caption="Higher and higher"
>}}

So we climb further and further through the mountains, the snow gets deeper and deeper - metres of it - and there are skiers all around us. We cross the Hochtannberg Pass and eventually reach Häselgehr. The pitch there is still full of snow too.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-zurueck-oesterreich/lechtal-02.jpeg"
  alt="Deeply snow-covered pitch in the Lech valley"
  caption="The pitch is still full of snow"
>}}

The absolute highlight is that our daughter comes to visit from the nearby Allgäu, and we hike up the mountain to the alpine hut together.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-zurueck-oesterreich/lechtal-03.jpeg"
  alt="Hike to the alpine hut in the Lech valley"
  caption="Up to the hut together"
>}}

## Inzell

We continue towards Schladming, but take the faster route through Germany and find a pitch on a farm in Inzell.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-zurueck-oesterreich/inzell-01.jpeg"
  alt="Farm pitch in Inzell"
  caption="A pitch at the forestry farm"
>}}

Forestry is the business here, so the conversation turns to trucks and stays there for a good long while. There is always a topic.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-zurueck-oesterreich/inzell-02.jpeg"
  alt="Landscape around Inzell"
  caption="Inzell"
>}}

Saskia uses my working hours for a hike through the local moorland.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-zurueck-oesterreich/inzell-03.jpeg"
  alt="Moorland near Inzell"
  caption="Through the moor"
>}}

Over dinner we then watch a driver overtake a blind man on the pavement, pull up directly in front of him and fling the passenger door open. Some people.

The next day we head on to Schladming.
