## Hello Sweden

For those who remember: back in Steinkjer we faced the decision - north, west or east. Now we are back at that point.

First we went north, then west to coastal road 17. Now it is the turn of the east, and we cross the border into Sweden. Our destination: the famous [Vildmarksvägen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vildmarksv%C3%A4gen).

## On the Vildmarksvägen

The well-known panoramic road takes us further and further up into the Swedish mountains and onto the high plateau - and naturally we make a few stops along the way.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-schweden-vildmarks/vildmarksvaegen-01.jpeg"
  alt="The Vildmarksvägen in Sweden"
  caption="Vildmarksvägen"
>}}

The first is an impressive waterfall. Sadly there are no walking paths there, so we can only enjoy the view from the viewpoints.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-schweden-vildmarks/vildmarksvaegen-02.jpeg"
  alt="Mountain landscape on the Vildmarksvägen"
  caption="Higher and higher"
>}}

The second stop is a ski area with a beautiful walking route past streams, small lakes and through moorland. Very much our sort of thing. And finally the high plateau itself.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-schweden-vildmarks/vildmarksvaegen-03.jpeg"
  alt="High plateau on the Vildmarksvägen"
  caption="Up on the high plateau"

## Hällingsåfallet

A little off the Vildmarksvägen - after a few kilometres of gravel - lies the Hällingsåfallet.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-schweden-vildmarks/haellingsafallet-01.jpeg"
  alt="The Hällingsåfallet waterfall"
  caption="Hällingsåfallet"
>}}

Yes, there are plenty of waterfalls in Scandinavia - but this one is among those worth seeing. The canyon the water has carved out here is particularly impressive.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-schweden-vildmarks/haellingsafallet-02.jpeg"
  alt="Canyon at the Hällingsåfallet"
  caption="The canyon is the impressive part"
>}}

We walk a few kilometres downstream and then back up again.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-schweden-vildmarks/haellingsafallet-03.jpeg"
  alt="Walking route at the Hällingsåfallet"
  caption="Downstream and back up"
>}}

In the car park we meet another expedition truck - contact details are exchanged immediately. And the gravel track combined with light rain means Mammothon is considerably less clean afterwards than it was before.
