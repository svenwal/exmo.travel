## Back on the Mediterranean

We are back in France and make two stops on the Mediterranean straight away.

The first takes us to the Plage du Lydia. There is a particular mixture here: a view of snow-covered mountains, a harbour, a beach - and of course the famous ship Lydia.

## Up the Rhône

We continue north along the [Rhône](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rh%C3%B4ne). We find a small pitch right behind the dyke - quiet, secluded and exactly our sort of thing.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-suedfrankreich-rhone/comps-occitanie-01.jpeg"
  alt="Pitch behind the dyke on the Rhône"
  caption="Right behind the dyke"
>}}

While I get on with the working day, Saskia sets off on a hike: down to the lock, across the river and then up to an old castle ruin.

## Aubignas

A little away from the Rhône valley we find a remote small municipal pitch at Aubignas.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-suedfrankreich-rhone/aubignas-01.jpeg"
  alt="The medieval village of Aubignas"
  caption="Aubignas"

The view of this beautiful medieval village - by day and by night alike - combined with complete quiet: a dream.
