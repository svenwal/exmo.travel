## One year done

We are closing out our first year with Mammothon. The first season of our new life is complete.

We leave Austria, but stop just before the border at the [Highline 179](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highline179). We were here a few years ago - back then I preferred to keep both feet on solid ground.

## Home holiday

After a year on the road we take a short home holiday - not without a few stops along the way, of course.

The first takes us to a small, comfortable pitch in the middle of the Nuthe-Nieplitz nature park, right on the edge of the forest.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-05-london-heimaturlaub/heimaturlaub-01.jpeg"
  alt="Pitch in the Nuthe-Nieplitz nature park"
  caption="Right on the forest edge"
>}}

Next we head to the gypsum quarries near Sperenberg. There we park right by the lake on the grounds of the former dog club where we ourselves used to compete years ago.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-05-london-heimaturlaub/heimaturlaub-02.jpeg"
  alt="Lake at the gypsum quarries in Sperenberg"
  caption="Sperenberg - we have been here before"
>}}

The third stop is Dabendorf, the neighbouring village to the place where our big journey began.

## London

To round off Season One we treat ourselves to one extra outing and fly to London for a few days. The occasion is a birthday - but naturally it does not stop at a good dinner.
