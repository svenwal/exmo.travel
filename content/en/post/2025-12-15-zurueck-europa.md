## Heading north

Now we head back towards Europe: once more across Marrakesh, then past Casablanca and Rabat, and finally on to Tanger Med.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-zurueck-europa/tanger-med-01.jpeg"
  alt="On the road towards Tanger Med"
  caption="One more run across the country"
>}}

It is not only sad to be leaving this fantastic country again - a small run of bad luck also begins here.

Part one is the entrance to the campsite we stop at along the way. We are cheerfully waved in - until something crunches and their nice gate now has a hole in it. A hundred percent their mistake, but unpleasant all the same.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-zurueck-europa/tanger-med-02.jpeg"
  alt="Stopover on the way to Tanger Med"
  caption="A stop along the way"
>}}

Part two we notice at the campsite: the batteries are not fully charged. Odd - and the start of a fault-finding exercise that will keep us busy for a while.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-zurueck-europa/tanger-med-04.jpeg"
  alt="Port area at Tanger Med"
  caption="Tanger Med"
>}}

## Thirty hours late

After a night at the port and a long wait at the ticket desk, we get our ferry - roughly 30 hours behind schedule.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-zurueck-europa/almeria-01.jpeg"
  alt="Waiting at the port"
  caption="Waiting is part of it"
>}}

But it gets more interesting. On the ramp up to the ferry, Mammothon's electronics decide that is quite enough. Why? The angle? The throttle? Magic? The gearbox shifts to N and stays there.

Not a pleasant feeling - particularly not with marshals watching and a queue of vehicles behind you.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-zurueck-europa/almeria-02.jpeg"
  alt="Driving up onto the ferry"
  caption="Here of all places"
>}}

In the end it turns out to be nothing but a computer. Everything off, key out, key in - and just like that, the gears are back.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-zurueck-europa/almeria-04.jpeg"
  alt="Arriving in Almería"
  caption="Back in Europe"
>}}

Thanks to the delay we reach [Almería](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almer%C3%ADa) considerably later than planned. But we are back in Europe - and the rest can be sorted out.
