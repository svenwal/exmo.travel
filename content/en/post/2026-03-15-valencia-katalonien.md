## Valencia

Valencia is always worth a visit. This is already our third time here.

Once again coincidence catches us out: it is [Las Fallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falles), the festival welcoming spring. The city is full of sculptures, and there is no shortage of fireworks either.

{{< figure

I fly out on business again and manage to run into the Lufthansa strike while I am at it - on the plus side, this time there are aerial shots.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-valencia-katalonien/valencia-04.jpeg"
  alt="View across Valencia"
  caption="Valencia from above"
>}}

Leaving involves a bit of shopping: IKEA, Decathlon, the supermarket - and the tyre dealer. The front wheels are now nicely new as well.

## Fog near Barcelona

For the next stop we have again picked somewhere remote in nature, this time a little outside Barcelona in the Parc Natural de Sant Llorenç del Munt i l'Obac.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-valencia-katalonien/sant-llorenc-01.jpeg"
  alt="Foggy mountain in the Parc Natural de Sant Llorenç"
  caption="This mountain, of all of them"
>}}

Perfect weather for the entire drive - except the exact mountain we end up on, which is buried in thick fog and rain.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-valencia-katalonien/sant-llorenc-02.jpeg"
  alt="Path in the fog near Sant Llorenç"
  caption="Paths barely recognisable as paths"
>}}

So it becomes an adventurous climb up the mountain, on routes that really cannot be identified as paths. Because of the cloud we can only guess at how beautiful it must be up there.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-valencia-katalonien/sant-llorenc-04.jpeg"
  alt="Fog-covered landscape near Barcelona"
  caption="Maybe next time"
>}}

If we are ever back in the region and the weather cooperates, we will definitely return.

## Last stop in Spain

One last stop in Spain: we are parked on the Costa Brava right by the sea. And we realise that a few years ago we stayed only a few hundred metres away with the caravan.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-valencia-katalonien/aiguamolls-01.jpeg"
  alt="Pitch on the Costa Brava"
  caption="Right by the sea"
>}}

Right next to us lies a nature reserve full of birds - flamingos here too.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-valencia-katalonien/aiguamolls-02.jpeg"
  alt="Aiguamolls de l'Empordà nature reserve"
  caption="Aiguamolls de l'Empordà"
>}}

The rainfall of recent months has put everything under water, though. Some paths are closed, and the one we take would in places have been better tackled in wellies.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-valencia-katalonien/aiguamolls-04.jpeg"
  alt="Flooded paths in the nature reserve"
  caption="Wellies would have been the better choice"

We are going to miss Spain.
