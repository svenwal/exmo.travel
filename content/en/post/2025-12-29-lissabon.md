## Constantly up and down

We have reached our destination for the holidays and are now in [Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon). On a "proper" campsite for the first time in a long while - and from here we explore the city on foot.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-lissabon/lissabon-01.jpeg"
  alt="View across Lisbon"
  caption="Lisbon"
>}}

Anyone who knows Lisbon knows: it is constantly up and down. No wonder, then, that there are funiculars and lifts right in the middle of the city.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-12-lissabon/lissabon-02.jpeg"
  alt="Street in Lisbon"

The funiculars have sadly been out of service since a serious accident last year. As a photographic subject they are still beautiful.

The actual reason for coming to a big city, though, is the airport. For Christmas we fly to the Allgäu to visit our daughter - and get a genuinely white Christmas there.
