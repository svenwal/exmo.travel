## From Brindisi to Igoumenitsa

Today we say goodbye to [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy): we take the [ferry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferry) from [Brindisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brindisi) to [Igoumenitsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igoumenitsa) in [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece). And, completely by chance, we are not travelling alone: On Polarsteps we happened to notice that the previous owner of Mammothon, back when it was still called "Charlotte", has exactly the same plan.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-09-griechenland-kefalonia/brindisi-hafen-fort.jpeg"
  alt="View across the harbour basin of Brindisi to a fort on a promontory"
  caption="Farewell to Brindisi"
>}}

Getting Mammothon onto the ferry means driving up several ramps until we stand on the very top deck. Basically a floating [multi-storey car park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-storey_car_park).

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-09-griechenland-kefalonia/faehre-oberdeck-lkw.jpeg"
  alt="View from the top deck of the ferry across the stern ramp to many parked trucks and the open sea"
  caption="The car park from above"
>}}

The crossing then unexpectedly turns into a long chat about Mammothon, travelling and everything that has happened since the truck changed hands. Another couple travelling on the same ferry joins us too. Meanwhile, Lou finds two fellow dogs on deck and is perfectly content.

And believe it or not, I even manage to get some important, time-critical work done. There is good [internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet) on board, for a fee of course, so I can finish a complete test system for a customer, built entirely from scratch. Quite possibly the most productive ferry crossing ever.

## Igoumenitsa

We arrive in Greece deep in the night. Antje shows us a small harbour where we can spend our first night, and there is not much left to do except sleep.

The next morning we see where we ended up: a quiet bay, mountains in the background, a pebble beach. Then our paths separate again, at least for now. Greece, here we are!

## Tourlida

Looking for a [Lidl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lidl) somewhere between us and [Patras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patras), we drive south for a good two hours, with regular views of the [sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea). After stocking up, the question is where to spend the night, and the answer is Tourlida: a parking area with the sea on one side and the [salt pans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_pan) on the other. It feels like we are parked right in the middle of the water.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-09-griechenland-kefalonia/tourlida-salinen-kirche.jpeg"
  alt="Mammothon between trees behind the salt pans, a yellow church with a blue dome on the right"
  caption="Salt pans, church and Mammothon"
>}}

And of course it is time for our first proper Greek meal. Hello [tzatziki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tzatziki), hello [garlic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garlic)! And yes, it is delicious.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-09-griechenland-kefalonia/tourlida-fischersteg.jpeg"
  alt="Wooden jetty between small fishing boats with a light-coloured dog standing on it"
  caption="The fishing jetty of Tourlida"
>}}

The wonderfully warm sea, by the way we are on the [Ionian Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ionian_Sea), practically begs us to jump in. In my case it even becomes a rather effective way to wake up and cool down the next morning. Afterwards there is a fresh-water shower right at the spot. And in the salt pans stand [flamingos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flamingo), which, to be honest, were not on our list.

  src="/images/blog/2026-09-griechenland-kefalonia/tourlida-flamingos.jpeg"

{{< figure

Greece, you are off to a very good start.

## Patras and the Ferry to Kefalonia

On we go to our planned intermediate destination: [Kefalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kefalonia). Near [Patras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patras) we first cross a rather imposing bridge, the [Rio-Antirrio Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio%E2%80%93Antirrio_Bridge), and continue to the ferry port.

{{< figure

On the way we pass the large [Church of Saint Andrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Saint_Andrew,_Patras), and then it is almost three and a half hours over the water, to be precise to [Sami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sami,_Cephalonia).

## Sami on Kefalonia

For the next two weeks we have booked a [holiday house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacation_rental) in [Sami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sami,_Cephalonia) on [Kefalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kefalonia). Mammothon gets its own pitch right in front of the house, and we first have to get used to this completely unfamiliar life with several rooms, doors and even different floors.
