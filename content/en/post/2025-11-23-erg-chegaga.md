## Getting stuck on purpose

Today we are at the famous dunes of [Erg Chegaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erg_Chebbi). I can only repeat myself about how superb this environment is - and how hard it is to get it onto a photograph.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-erg-chegaga/erg-chegaga-01.jpeg"
  alt="Dunes of Erg Chegaga"
  caption="Erg Chegaga"
>}}

But we are not only here for fun, we are here to learn something. So we deliberately get several vehicles stuck - all diff locks off, wrong tyre pressure, full throttle - and then recover them in various different ways.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-erg-chegaga/erg-chegaga-02.jpeg"
  alt="Vehicle in the sand at Erg Chegaga"
  caption="Getting stuck takes practice too"
>}}

We are the demonstration vehicle for recovery using sand ladders. It works excellently, and the ladders do not get bent in the slightest - provided somebody explains the tricks to you first.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-erg-chegaga/erg-chegaga-04.jpeg"
  alt="Dune landscape at Erg Chegaga"
  caption="And afterwards, simply looking"
>}}

## The last training point

The day starts once again with wonderful views. The outlook from up on the dune in particular sits very high on my personal list.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-erg-chegaga/allougoum-01.jpeg"
  alt="View from the dune in the morning"
  caption="The view from the dune"
>}}

Afterwards we head to the final training point: vehicle checks and maintenance. The cabs get tilted, temperatures measured, filters cleaned and visual inspections carried out.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-erg-chegaga/allougoum-02.jpeg"
  alt="Maintenance work on the vehicles"
  caption="Cab tilted, filters cleaned"
>}}

Then we find ourselves a spot among the rocks. Another excellent photo location.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-erg-chegaga/allougoum-04.jpeg"
  alt="Pitch among the rocks"
  caption="A spot among the rocks"
>}}

## Aït-Ben-Haddou

We move back towards civilisation again. First to a bazaar, then on to the tourist hotspot of [Aït-Ben-Haddou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%AFt_Benhaddou).

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-erg-chegaga/ait-ben-haddou-01.jpeg"
  alt="The mud-brick town of Aït-Ben-Haddou"
  caption="Aït-Ben-Haddou"
>}}

This old mud-brick town now consists largely of souvenir shops, and the streets around it are once again full of cars. Even so - or perhaps because of it - it is a wonderful place.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-erg-chegaga/ait-ben-haddou-02.jpeg"
  alt="Alleyways in Aït-Ben-Haddou"
  caption="In the alleyways"
>}}

It is also familiar from a number of films and series - it stands in for Essos in *Game of Thrones*, and in *Gladiator* it is the arena after his arrest.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-erg-chegaga/ait-ben-haddou-04.jpeg"
  alt="View of Aït-Ben-Haddou"
  caption="From a little further away"
>}}

And there is plenty of choice when it comes to food here too. Vegetarian tajine has by now become Saskia's favourite dish.
