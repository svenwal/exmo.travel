## Cleaning up first

After some genuinely exhausting days both we and Mammothon need a break. So we head for the area around [Lleida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lleida) - and make straight for a truck wash. We had collected "a little bit" of dirt over the preceding days.

  src="/images/blog/2025-10-pyrenaeen-katalonien/aitona-lleida-01.jpeg"

The two extremely friendly guys at the wash cannot stop telling us how much they love the truck. That happens to us more or less everywhere, incidentally.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-10-pyrenaeen-katalonien/aitona-lleida-02.jpeg"
  alt="Mammothon freshly washed"

With a gleaming Mammothon we then spend two nights at a campsite near Aitona - simply to wind down and enjoy the first Spanish tapas.

## Monegros - and a crooked solar panel

Looking for remote places, we push on straight through a storm and into the semi-desert of the [Monegros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monegros_Desert). Once there we squeeze Mammothon into a very tight gap under some trees.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-10-pyrenaeen-katalonien/half-desert-huesca-01.jpeg"
  alt="Semi-desert of the Monegros"
  caption="Monegros"
>}}

The real shock only comes when we open the roof hatch: one of our solar panels is hanging completely crooked. The wind was strong enough to bend the aluminium mount.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-10-pyrenaeen-katalonien/half-desert-huesca-02.jpeg"
  alt="Landscape in the Monegros semi-desert"
  caption="The wind really moves through up here"
>}}

It looks genuinely bad - fortunately it turns out not to be that hard to bend everything back. Another lesson learned: extremely strong wind plus motorway is a rather unfortunate combination.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-10-pyrenaeen-katalonien/half-desert-huesca-04.jpeg"
  alt="Out in the Monegros"
  caption="Monegros"
>}}

## Ebro delta

Central Spain via Madrid, or the coast after all? We ask ourselves that question - and a few Spanish friends along with it.

In the end it becomes a complete change of scene: from the semi-desert of the Monegros to the green [Ebro delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebro_Delta).

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-10-pyrenaeen-katalonien/ebro-delta-01.jpeg"
  alt="In the nature reserve of the Ebro delta"
  caption="Ebro delta"
>}}

We walk right around the L'Encanyissada lagoon, spot countless birds - flamingos included - and find a genuinely good restaurant in the middle of this beautiful nature reserve.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-10-pyrenaeen-katalonien/ebro-delta-02.jpeg"
  alt="Lagoon in the Ebro delta"
  caption="Around the lagoon"
>}}

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-10-pyrenaeen-katalonien/ebro-delta-04.jpeg"
  alt="Our pitch in the Ebro delta"
  caption="This pitch is worth the detour"
>}}

This free pitch is definitely worth the detour if you are on the coast road. And the funniest part: even weeks later we keep meeting campers who saw us there at exactly the same time. The overlanding world is small.
