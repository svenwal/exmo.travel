## Hollywood in Europe

Hollywood in Europe? As it turns out, yes. Many of the best known desert scenes in film history were shot in the [Tabernas desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabernas_Desert) - and that is exactly where we are now.

  src="/images/blog/2025-11-tabernas-almeria/tabernas-01.jpeg"

  caption="Tabernas desert"
>}}

We hike through impressive canyons and recognise several filming locations along the way. The landscape is simply spectacular.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-tabernas-almeria/tabernas-02.jpeg"
  alt="Canyon in the Tabernas desert"

And the icing on the cake: our pitch has a restaurant done up in proper diner style. Fitting.

## Last day in Europe

We arrive in the port of [Almería](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almer%C3%ADa) and meet the other eight trucks we will be travelling through Morocco with for the next 17 days.

  src="/images/blog/2025-11-tabernas-almeria/almeria-01.jpeg"
  alt="Trucks in the port of Almería"

The mood is a mixture of anticipation, excitement and a little nervousness - because tomorrow the next level begins.

## Onto the ferry

Today we board the ferry. We may be a truck, but we are still directed down the steep, narrow ramp into the car and camper deck.

Rule number one on a ferry: do not blindly trust the marshal. We learned that one with our first caravan on the way to Norway.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-tabernas-almeria/marokko-01.jpeg"
  alt="On the ferry to Morocco"
  caption="Ferry day"
>}}

The next morning we are there - hello [Morocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morocco). And then we cover ground: first east along the Mediterranean, then south.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-tabernas-almeria/marokko-02.jpeg"
  alt="First kilometres in Morocco"
  caption="The first kilometres in Morocco"
>}}

We make two stops for SIM cards and a supermarket before slowly reaching the first more remote areas.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-tabernas-almeria/marokko-04.jpeg"
  alt="Landscape in northern Morocco"
  caption="It slowly gets quieter"
>}}

Because of how close we are to the border, we sleep directly in front of a police station. Afterwards we walk around the small town.
