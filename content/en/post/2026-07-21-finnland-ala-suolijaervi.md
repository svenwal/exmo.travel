## A detour north

When planning our trip through [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland), Sven had hoped to visit a colleague and friend in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki). Unfortunately he was on holiday at exactly that time - at his remote cabin by a lake in the middle of [Lapland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lapland_%28Finland%29).

So the sat nav is reprogrammed on the spot and we head north once more, back towards the [Arctic Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_Circle). The lake is called [Ala-Suolijärvi](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ala-Suolij%C3%A4rvi), lies once again in the municipality of [Posio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Posio) and is, after all, the seventy-second largest lake in Finland - which, in a country with more than one hundred and eighty thousand lakes, is a remarkably precise figure.

The last kilometres are then quite something: gravel tracks getting narrower and narrower, into the forest and at some point into the middle of nowhere. This is exactly how we had imagined Lapland.

## The jetty

And Sven gets to make himself useful straight away: together with the whole family he helps build a new jetty. The old one does not reach far enough out into the lake, so it gets extended - in wellies, standing in the water, with boards, screws and the usual amount of discussion about the correct order of operations.

## Lake and berries

While Sven works, Saskia and Lou explore the surroundings their own way: with a boat trip on the lake. That afternoon it lies so still that the horizon is nothing but a thin green line between two skies.

On land we carry on on foot. The paths here are narrow sandy tracks through heather and low pine forest, and now and then you briefly wonder whether this is still a path or just a spot where nothing happens to grow.

And along the way there is picking to be done: [bilberries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilberry) and [crowberries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empetrum) grow everywhere here among the [heather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calluna). Lou has a clear methodological advantage in this: she is already at eye level with the berries anyway.
