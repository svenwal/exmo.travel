## Ronda

Today we head for one of Andalusia's best known highlights: the town of [Ronda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronda) with its "New Bridge". Hardly any subject from this region turns up more often.

We walk right across the town twice. As well as visiting the bridge, we work our way down the path to the abandoned building below.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-ronda-torcal/ronda-02.jpeg"
  alt="Gorge below the bridge in Ronda"

Not forgetting the journey there, with two stops along the way, one of them in complete seclusion.

## El Torcal de Antequera

The next destination on our trip across Spain is the [El Torcal de Antequera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Torcal_de_Antequera) nature reserve.

The approach to the visitor centre car park was closed at times, though. So we drive on a few hundred metres further and find a small pull-in right beside a spring.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-ronda-torcal/el-torcal-01.jpeg"
  alt="Climbing in the El Torcal nature reserve"
  caption="Walking boots on and upwards"
>}}

From there it is walking boots on and up the mountain. We meet only a handful of people along the way - but at the big car park at the top it is suddenly busy again.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-ronda-torcal/el-torcal-02.jpeg"
  alt="Karst formations in El Torcal"
  caption="El Torcal de Antequera"
>}}

Up there we find two circular routes, one easy and one moderate. The rock formations here really are extraordinary - you do not see this sort of thing often.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-ronda-torcal/el-torcal-04.jpeg"
  alt="Rocks in El Torcal de Antequera"
  caption="Layer upon layer"
>}}

## Sierra de Huétor

More driving days again. Because of work we only get a little further each day - but that is exactly why we keep discovering new places.

The first stop takes us into the [Sierra de Huétor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_Hu%C3%A9tor) nature park: a remote pitch in the middle of the forest. At this time of year we have it almost entirely to ourselves.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-ronda-torcal/sierra-de-huetor-01.jpeg"
  alt="Forest in the Sierra de Huétor nature park"
  caption="Almost alone in the forest"
>}}

Even on the way in you notice how fiercely the recent storms raged here. Fallen trees lie across the forest everywhere.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-ronda-torcal/sierra-de-huetor-02.jpeg"
  alt="Fallen trees in the forest of the Sierra de Huétor"
  caption="The storms did a thorough job"
>}}

A large part of the car park is closed off as a result - but there is still a suitable spot for us.
