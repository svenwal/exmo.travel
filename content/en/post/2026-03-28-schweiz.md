## Bern

Time for a new flag. We had actually intended to stay another two or three days in the Rhône valley - but it is pouring. So instead we cover ground and drive, with a short stop in Lausanne, to [Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bern).

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-schweiz/bern-01.jpeg"
  alt="Old town of Bern"
  caption="Bern"
>}}

The semi-official Swiss capital - officially the "federal city" - had already won me over on various business visits. Now there was finally time to explore it properly and show Saskia and Lou a few corners.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-schweiz/bern-02.jpeg"
  alt="Alleyways in Bern's old town"
  caption="Time to take it in properly"
>}}

Then, while I got back to work, Saskia hiked up the Gurten. The funicular was of course ignored entirely.

## Lake Lucerne

From Zurich we head, with a short stop in Thun, over the Brünig Pass to [Lake Lucerne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Lucerne).

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-schweiz/buochs-vierwaldstaetter-see-01.jpeg"
  alt="Lake Lucerne near Buochs"
  caption="Lake Lucerne"
>}}

We notice immediately that we have got ourselves fairly deep into the mountains - and so try winter camping with Mammothon for the first time, more or less by accident.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-schweiz/buochs-vierwaldstaetter-see-02.jpeg"
  alt="Snow at Lake Lucerne"
  caption="Winter camping, unplanned"
>}}

Saskia does a long loop out onto the nearby peninsula and has to fight her way through the snow. At the weekend we walk the route again together - this time without snow, but still demanding enough.
