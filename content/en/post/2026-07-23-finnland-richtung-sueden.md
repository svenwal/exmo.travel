## Change of course

After weeks in which the needle pointed further and further north, the direction from now on is: south. We have a workshop appointment at Krug in [Schladming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schladming), and there are still a few thousand kilometres to cover before then. So we say goodbye to Sven's friend at the [Arctic Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_Circle) and set off on the long way back towards Austria.

For us, though, a long way does not mean driving it in one go, but three stopovers of a few days each - Sven's working week happens on the road as well, after all.

## Kylmäluoma

The first stop is Kylmäluoma, an area of lakes and hiking trails not far from [Kuusamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuusamo). We are put up at a youth centre and park Mammothon among the pines, a few steps from the water.

In the usual manner Saskia explores the surroundings while Sven gets on with his working day. The paths here are soft sandy tracks through open pine forest - and Lou sets the pace.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-finnland-richtung-sueden/kylmaluoma-wanderweg.jpeg"
  alt="Lou standing in the middle of a sandy forest track between tall pines, looking into the camera"
  caption="A signpost on four legs"

At some point the forest opens up and the path continues as a narrow boardwalk straight across a [bog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bog). Two planks wide, kilometres long, and nothing to the left and right but cotton grass and the occasional pine.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-finnland-richtung-sueden/kylmaluoma-moor.jpeg"
  alt="Narrow boardwalk of two planks leading straight across a wide bog under a blue sky"
  caption="Two planks across the bog"
>}}

At the end of it there is a visibly ageing jetty across a bog pond. Lou goes first and declares it fine.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-finnland-richtung-sueden/kylmaluoma-steg.jpeg"
  alt="Old wooden jetty with a crooked railing crossing a dark bog pond, Lou standing in the middle of it"
  caption="The jetty has seen better days"
>}}

In the evening there is a special surprise: the canteen makes [pizza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza) especially for us. After weeks largely without a restaurant that feels like a banquet.

Speaking of cooking: this is where we get to know a Finnish achievement we had been sorely missing without knowing it - ready-crushed [garlic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garlic) in a jar. *Valkosipulimurska*. That definitely has to come home with us, or rather into Mammothon.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-finnland-richtung-sueden/kylmaluoma-knoblauch.jpeg"
  alt="Opened jar of crushed garlic with a spoon in it, labelled Valkosipulimurska"
  caption="Probably the most important Finnish invention"
>}}

## Suomussalmi

The next stop is [Suomussalmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suomussalmi). We stand by the water, between boat jetties and birches, and in the evening we have the best view in the whole village.

  src="/images/blog/2026-07-finnland-richtung-sueden/suomussalmi-stellplatz.jpeg"

>}}

{{< figure

Finland keeps going on the culinary front here too: salad on the pizza. It sounds unusual at first, but it tastes so good that we will definitely be trying it ourselves in future.

On foot we explore the village and find a white timber truss bridge, a surprisingly deep rock gorge in the middle of the forest and a decommissioned steam locomotive quietly rusting away at the edge of town.

## North of Kuopio

Our last stopover is near Toivala, a little to the north of [Kuopio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuopio). We stand right by the water, in a place where no tourist would normally come past - and certainly not with a sixteen-tonne Mammothon.

That is of course a talking point, and so once again we have a number of lovely encounters with the locals.

The forest here suddenly looks completely different from [Lapland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lapland_%28Finland%29): no more pine heath, but dense [birch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch) woodland over waist-high ferns, with a narrow boardwalk running through it.

With that we have almost crossed Finland - and we are on schedule for Schladming. At least up to this point.
