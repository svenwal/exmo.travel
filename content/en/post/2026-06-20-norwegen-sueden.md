## Off to Norway

After a few lovely days in Denmark, the crossing to Norway is next. Like so many travellers we take the classic route from Hirtshals to Kristiansand.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-sueden/vennesla-01.jpeg"
  alt="Beach near Hirtshals"
  caption="Denmark one last time"

This time we gave ourselves a bit more time in Hirtshals - and were rewarded. West of the town we find a beautiful beach with a comfortable campsite and an ice cream shop whose selection makes the decision anything but easy.

  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-sueden/vennesla-02.jpeg"

## Nordberg Fort

Since we are now staying unexpectedly longer at the campsite in Mandal, we start by enjoying the fine beach right outside the door. You have to make the best of every situation.

Afterwards we continue to Nordberg Fort.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-sueden/nordberg-fort-01.jpeg"
  alt="Cultural landscape at Nordberg Fort"
  caption="Nordberg Fort"

From there we explore a landscape shaped by people over thousands of years, and visit the remains of the German fortifications from the Second World War.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-sueden/nordberg-fort-02.jpeg"

## Flekkefjord

Today one of the highlights of our Norway trip is on the agenda - although: is there really such a thing as *the* highlight in Norway? Here one dream location seems to follow another.

This time we head to Flekkefjord, for a ride on a rail bike along the old railway line.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-sueden/flekkefjord-01.jpeg"
  alt="Old railway line near Flekkefjord"
  caption="The old railway line"
>}}

What an experience. The route climbs steadily, through countless pitch-dark, damp tunnels and onwards to the end of the line. Giving up is, after all, not an option.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-sueden/flekkefjord-02.jpeg"
  alt="Tunnel on the rail bike route"
  caption="Pitch dark and damp"
>}}

Right by the harbour we then find a large pitch.
