## Fredrika

The [Vildmarksvägen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vildmarksv%C3%A4gen) is behind us, and with it the mountainous west of Sweden. Now we head east, straight across [Västerbotten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4sterbotten) to the [Gulf of Bothnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Bothnia). Two stopovers lie on the way.

The first is [Fredrika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredrika,_Sweden), where we settle onto a small campsite - and in such a way that we are surrounded by water on both sides. The big lake in front, a small pond behind. So the only decision left is which way to point the chairs.

## Tavelsjö

The second stopover is [Tavelsjö](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tavelsj%C3%B6), and in the order of things it starts out unspectacularly: first of all we park on a trailhead car park. It has mobile coverage, and stable coverage is exactly what Sven needs for his online meetings.

For the night we move directly to the local swimming beach. There is not only water at the doorstep but also pizza from a wood-fired oven - and it really is very good. Not something you expect in a place this size.

The next day it is straight back to the trailhead car park: Sven has more online meetings, Saskia does a second round on the hill. A division of labour that works well for both sides.

## Umeå

The day ends in [Umeå](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ume%C3%A5), the largest city in northern Sweden. First stop: [IKEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IKEA), because our hot dog supplies are running low. Restocking works out - anything else would have been a disappointment at an IKEA in Sweden. After that a quick run to the supermarket for the rest of the supplies.

For the night then the city campsite - and once again it is really not our thing: huge, full, cramped and expensive to make up for it. After two nights with a lake view and a beach view, that takes some getting used to.
