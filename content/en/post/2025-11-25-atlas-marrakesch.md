## Over the pass

Now we really are leaving the wild country south of the Atlas, because today we cross the [High Atlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlas_Mountains) and drive to Marrakesh.

The route runs partly through a narrow valley and climbs a series of hairpins. At the top of the pass we take a short photo stop in cold wind - we had almost forgotten what cold even is over the last few days.

Afterwards the roads get busier and busier until we are in the traffic chaos of [Marrakesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marrakesh). First a shopping mall for fresh supplies, then our last shared stopping place as a group, in the south of the city.

## The last day of the tour

Today is essentially a lie-in: no training, no long drives. Simply waking up in sunshine at the campsite and doing nothing for a while.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-atlas-marrakesch/marrakesch-01.jpeg"
  alt="Campsite near Marrakesh"
  caption="A day with no programme"
>}}

Our group does have one final item on the agenda though: a visit to the medina, followed by a farewell dinner.

So we head towards the city centre in the trucks, complete with Marrakesh-style traffic, and line ourselves up neatly in a car park - with the marshal's blessing. We have barely finished when the police turn up and send us away again: because of a police congress, the whole area is a security zone.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-atlas-marrakesch/marrakesch-02.jpeg"
  alt="Out and about in Marrakesh"
  caption="Parking in Marrakesh, attempt one"
>}}

Back at the campsite the evening turns comfortable after all - and it makes a good close to 17 days together.
