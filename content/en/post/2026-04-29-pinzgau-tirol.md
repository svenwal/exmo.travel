## Wald im Pinzgau

We are finally on the road again. Our route takes us right across Austria and eventually into the Pinzgau, where we set up camp in the small village of Wald im Pinzgau.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-pinzgau-tirol/wald-im-pinzgau-01.jpeg"
  alt="The Krimml Waterfalls"
  caption="Krimml Waterfalls"
>}}

From here we take on three larger hikes straight away. The first goes to the [Krimml Waterfalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krimml_Waterfalls) - and not just the falls themselves, but the walk out from Wald and back as well.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-pinzgau-tirol/wald-im-pinzgau-02.jpeg"
  alt="Mountain landscape in the Pinzgau"
  caption="Out in the Pinzgau"
>}}

The second climbs the Gernkogel - a serious outing, with some still impressive snowfields near the summit. But Saskia would not be Saskia if that stopped her.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-pinzgau-tirol/wald-im-pinzgau-03.jpeg"
  alt="Snowfields near the summit in the Pinzgau"
  caption="There is still snow at the top"
>}}

## Weer in Tyrol

Our last pitch in Austria. Before we head off on our "home holiday", we make one final stop in Weer in Tyrol.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-pinzgau-tirol/weer-tirol-01.jpeg"
  alt="Hairpin bends on the Gerlos Pass"
  caption="Over the Gerlos Pass"
>}}

The drive there goes over the Gerlos Pass - and working your way up and down through the Alps on hairpins is simply enjoyable every single time.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-pinzgau-tirol/weer-tirol-02.jpeg"
  alt="Landscape near Weer in Tyrol"
  caption="Weer, Tyrol"
>}}

In Weer things go the way they usually do: while I get on with the working day, Saskia explores the area on foot and diligently racks up more altitude.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-pinzgau-tirol/weer-tirol-03.jpeg"
  alt="View across the Inn valley near Weer"
  caption="Alpine air one more time"
>}}

Mountains once more, fresh alpine air once more, and one more chance to enjoy the quiet before the next chapter begins.
