## A hike with stairs

Quite how big this staircase actually is was, wisely, kept from me at first.

Our destination is the Midsundtrappene - one of the famous Norwegian stone staircases, and with over 3,000 steps in total, among the longest in the country.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-norwegen-trondheim/midsund-01.jpeg"
  alt="Stone staircase of the Midsundtrappene"
  caption="Midsundtrappene"
>}}

Hiking uphill is demanding enough on its own. But uphill on a staircase? Oof.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-norwegen-trondheim/midsund-02.jpeg"
  alt="Climbing the staircase near Midsund"
  caption="Uphill. On a staircase."
>}}

Giving up is of course still out of the question. Even after reaching the first summit and discovering that over 1,000 steps still lie ahead.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-norwegen-trondheim/midsund-03.jpeg"
  alt="View near Midsund"
  caption="And the view from the top"
>}}

## Atlantic Ocean Road

Today one of Norway's best known photo spots is on the agenda: the [Atlantic Ocean Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Ocean_Road). Here spectacular bridges link the small islands together, creating one of the most beautiful coastal roads in Europe.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-norwegen-trondheim/nordlandet-01.jpeg"
  alt="Bridge on the Atlantic Ocean Road"
  caption="Atlantic Ocean Road"
>}}

The crowds are correspondingly large - it feels like everyone wants to take exactly this one photo home with them.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-norwegen-trondheim/nordlandet-02.jpeg"
  alt="Coastal road with bridges in Norway"
  caption="Everybody wants the same photo"
>}}

After the bustle we are drawn back to where we feel most comfortable: a small trailhead car park in the middle of nowhere. Let us put it this way - large expedition trucks are presumably not an everyday sight there.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-norwegen-trondheim/nordlandet-03.jpeg"
  alt="Remote pitch in Norway"
  caption="Back to the middle of nowhere"
>}}

## Trondheim

[Trondheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trondheim) had until now always been a city we drove past. We have come through here several times on our way north - this time we finally take the time to explore it.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-norwegen-trondheim/trondheim-01.jpeg"
  alt="Nidaros Cathedral in Trondheim"
  caption="Nidaros Cathedral"
>}}

First on the list is of course [Nidaros Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nidaros_Cathedral). The imposing cathedral is not only the most significant church in Norway but also the final resting place of many Norwegian kings.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-07-norwegen-trondheim/trondheim-02.jpeg"
  alt="Historic warehouses in Trondheim"
  caption="The old warehouses"
>}}

Equally impressive is the historic harbour quarter with its old wooden warehouses right on the water.
