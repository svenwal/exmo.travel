## Mammutmarsch, round two

We planned Mammothon's maintenance appointment so that we could spend a week in [Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna) - because there is another Mammutmarsch here.

After the 30 kilometres in Madrid we move up a level and take on 50.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-wien-schladming/wien-01.jpeg"
  alt="Out on the Mammutmarsch in Vienna"
  caption="Fifty kilometres ahead"
>}}

At around kilometre 25 we reach the Nasenweg. It climbs steeply for a fair few metres of elevation and then drops back down again. That slows us somewhat over the final ten kilometres - me in particular, since my feet are no longer entirely on speaking terms with me.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-wien-schladming/wien-02.jpeg"
  alt="View over Vienna from the Nasenweg"
  caption="The Nasenweg at kilometre 25"
>}}

But we reach the finish all the same, after roughly ten hours.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-wien-schladming/wien-03.jpeg"
  alt="Vienna in the evening"
  caption="Arrived after ten hours"
>}}

## Two weeks without Mammothon

While Mammothon is in the workshop at [Krug Expedition](https://krugxp.com/), Saskia uses the time to see Vienna properly - a musical included. I work out of the Airbnb in the meantime.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-wien-schladming/wien-und-schladming-01.jpeg"
  alt="Vienna in spring"
  caption="Time to actually see the city"
>}}

Staff shortages mean the work takes longer than planned, though. So we add a second week in an Airbnb in Schladming.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-wien-schladming/wien-und-schladming-02.jpeg"
  alt="Mountains around Schladming"
  caption="At least: more elevation to collect"
>}}

At least that means fresh opportunities to gain some altitude.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-04-wien-schladming/wien-und-schladming-03.jpeg"
  alt="Hiking near Schladming"
  caption="Schladming"
>}}

But honestly, two weeks without Mammothon drag considerably more than we had expected. A shower with unlimited hot water is lovely - but you miss your own home on wheels faster than you would think.
