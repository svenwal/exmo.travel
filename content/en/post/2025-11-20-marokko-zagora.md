## Fetching water - with an incident

Today we temporarily leave the track to refill our water supplies. But we have barely set off when we spot a trail of water on the track.

It quickly becomes clear: one vehicle has a broken coolant hose. So, a spontaneous repair in the middle of nowhere. Teamwork works perfectly out here too.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-zagora/zagora-01.jpeg"
  alt="Out on the track near Zagora"
  caption="Out near Zagora"
>}}

Next stop: bazaar time. Olives, spices and other good things get restocked - supplies for the days ahead.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-zagora/zagora-02.jpeg"
  alt="Bazaar in Zagora"
  caption="Supplies for the coming days"
>}}

Afterwards we reach a campsite in the palm grove of Amezrou: fresh water, shade and, once again, good food.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-zagora/zagora-04.jpeg"
  alt="Palms near Zagora"
  caption="Shade and fresh water"
>}}

## Into the crater

Another complete change of landscape. Today we drive into the Siroko-Mora crater - which means taking a rough track up over the rim first, then back down inside.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-zagora/ktaoua-01.jpeg"
  alt="Track over the crater rim"
  caption="Up first, then down"
>}}

We had trained precise manoeuvring beforehand in preparation. The track had recently been graded, though - making it only half as demanding as expected.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-zagora/ktaoua-02.jpeg"
  alt="Inside the crater"
  caption="Arrived inside the crater"
>}}

Once inside the crater, tyre change training is on the programme. Not to be confused with changing a wheel - a small but meaningful difference.

Afterwards we take up our spot right on the crater rim, with a fantastic view.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-zagora/ktaoua-04.jpeg"
  alt="Pitch on the crater rim"
  caption="Parked right on the crater rim"
>}}

## A shared dinner

After the tyre change training it is back over the stony track, out of the crater and into the desert again.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-zagora/ait-zineb-01.jpeg"
  alt="Stony track leading out of the crater"
  caption="Back out into the desert"
>}}

The main event of the day is then a shared dinner: everyone pushes their tables and chairs together and cooks whatever they feel like.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2025-11-marokko-zagora/ait-zineb-02.jpeg"
  alt="The group's vehicles in the desert"
  caption="All the tables pushed together"

It turns into a lovely, sociable evening - rounded off by another of the fantastic sunsets that are so typical here.
