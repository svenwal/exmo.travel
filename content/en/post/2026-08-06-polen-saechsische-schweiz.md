## Through the gap into Poland

We continue through the [Suwałki Gap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwa%C5%82ki_Gap) into [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland). Just past the border we make our first stop: stocking up on supplies and topping up the [diesel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_fuel) tank at local prices. Then it is out of the centre of [Suwałki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwa%C5%82ki) to a suburb, where we find a small pitch right by the lake. We spend our first night in [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) with water outside the door.

## Masuria

The next day we continue to the [Masurian Lake District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masurian_Lake_District). Near [Ukta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukta) we settle on a well-kept pitch set nicely in the green. And apparently the [storks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_stork) are firmly part of the inventory here: they simply walk across the site.

## Back in Germany

The last few days we covered a good distance: straight across [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland), and at [Görlitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%B6rlitz) it is back into [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany). Just before the border the [diesel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_fuel) tank is of course filled once more. Barely across the border another pitch of the slightly more unusual kind is waiting: an airfield.

From there we continue to [Saxon Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saxon_Switzerland). And of course the obligatory question at the reception comes up here too: "Are you sure that your vehicle fits on our site?" Yes. It fits.

A hike through the impressive rock landscape is of course a must. We set off for the Lampertsstein and enjoy the view from above. Narrow gaps and ladders lead up between the rocks, and we squeeze through them with backpack and dog. Lou bears it with dignity when she is carried once again.

## Bastei

The next day the weekend is unfortunately over again. So for Sven it is: laptop out and work, this time from the car park at the [Bastei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bastei). While Saskia looks at the famous tourist attraction, Sven turns Mammothon into a rolling office again. And by the way he even meets new [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram) followers in person.
