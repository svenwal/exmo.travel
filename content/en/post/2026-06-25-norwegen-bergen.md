## Over the pass to Rysstad

Today we cross one of the Norwegian passes, from Sinnes to Rysstad. As so often in Norway, the roads are mostly only about one and a half lanes wide. Oncoming traffic? No problem - as long as both sides stay alert.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/rysstad-01.jpeg"
  alt="Mountain pass road in Norway"
  caption="One and a half lanes, as so often"
>}}

Up at the Rosskreppfjorden we fit in a short walk. The view across the lake, countless sheep and the impressive mountain landscape make the detour more than worthwhile.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/rysstad-02.jpeg"
  alt="View across the Rosskreppfjorden"
  caption="Rosskreppfjorden"
>}}

Unfortunately a new noise also makes itself known here: a slipping belt. It will keep us company for a while yet.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/rysstad-03.jpeg"
  alt="Mountain landscape near Rysstad"
  caption="Rysstad"
>}}

## Bergen

[Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen) had been on our list for a long time. Although we have travelled through Norway several times, we had somehow always skipped the city. Now we finally put that right.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/bergen-01.jpeg"
  alt="Harbour in Bergen"
  caption="Bergen"
>}}

We find a pitch in the north of the city with good bus connections. Before any sightseeing, though, comes a workshop visit: that squealing belt finally needs looking at.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/bergen-02.jpeg"
  alt="Streets in Bergen"
  caption="Workshop first, city second"
>}}

Fortunately the cause is found quickly: a broken bolt holding our second alternator. After that we can finally turn our attention to the city.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/bergen-03.jpeg"
  alt="View across Bergen"
  caption="Time for the city at last"
>}}

## Ostereidet

Today it gets properly idyllic again. We find a small farm right at the end of a narrow road. The final stretch climbs a steep gravel track before we finally reach our pitch.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/ostereidet-01.jpeg"
  alt="Farm near Ostereidet"
  caption="Right at the end of the road"
>}}

In return we are rewarded with complete quiet. No traffic, no rush - just nature and the sounds of the farm. Exactly the sort of place we love finding out here.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/ostereidet-02.jpeg"
  alt="Landscape near Ostereidet"
  caption="Nothing but nature and farm sounds"
>}}

Naturally we do not leave empty-handed: there is homemade jam.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/ostereidet-03.jpeg"
  alt="Surroundings of the farm near Ostereidet"
  caption="Ostereidet"
>}}

## Briksdalsbreen

A circle closes: almost exactly ten years ago we stood at the [Briksdalsbreen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Briksdalsbreen) once before. Now we are back.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/briksdalsbreen-01.jpeg"
  alt="The Briksdalsbreen glacier"
  caption="Briksdalsbreen"
>}}

Our pitch is right on the lake, and we first set off towards the glacier on foot. Everything goes well as far as the car park - then we realise the last bus back to the campsite leaves too early. There would not have been enough time for the remaining stretch to the glacier.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/briksdalsbreen-02.jpeg"
  alt="Path to the Briksdalsbreen"
  caption="The path to the glacier"
>}}

So we make a second attempt the following day - this time taking the bus straight to the starting point.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-06-norwegen-bergen/briksdalsbreen-03.jpeg"
  alt="Lake at the Briksdalsbreen"
  caption="Pitch right on the lake"
>}}
