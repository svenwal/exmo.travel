## Crooked in the Ore Mountains

We continue to a very remote pitch in the [Ore Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ore_Mountains), found via Landvergnügen. The drive there is already exciting, and the pitch itself is then, let us say, quite crooked.

  caption="Mammothon parks steeply"

For the night we can at least park more or less level down by the road. The next morning, however, Mammothon has to go back up onto the sloping pitch. The reason: [Starlink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starlink). Only up there does Sven have a clear view of the sky for work. So he tries to get his workplace as level as possible while also finding a good connection to the satellite. Doing both at once works only so-so.

Meanwhile Saskia explores the surroundings as usual. She is out on the [Kammweg Erzgebirge-Vogtland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kammweg_Erzgebirge%E2%80%93Vogtland) and so almost at the [Czech border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic). On her hike she even made it briefly across the green border.

## Bayreuth

After fighting our way out of the [Ore Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ore_Mountains) through all the little roads, we continue to [Bayreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayreuth). There is a free motorhome pitch quite centrally here. You know the game by now: Sven works in Mammothon, Saskia explores the city centre.

[Bayreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayreuth) is of course the city of [Richard Wagner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Wagner), and he sits there as a bronze statue with crossed legs, as relaxed as if the festival season had never been stressful. Add to that the palaces of the [Margraves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margraviate_of_Brandenburg-Bayreuth) with their courtyards and the [Hofgarten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hofgarten,_Bayreuth), the town church and, quite unexpectedly, a [dinosaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinosaur) in the pedestrian zone.

## Truppach

We do not want to spend the night in [Bayreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayreuth) after all. The site is quite full and correspondingly restless. So after work we continue to Truppach-Mengersdorf, once again via Landvergnügen. Unfortunately the farm is a case of "everything is going down the drain here at the moment". You can still see very clearly that this must once have been a really great location, but that was apparently a few years ago.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-08-erzgebirge-bayreuth-mering/truppach-stellplatz.jpeg"
  alt="Mammothon parked on gravel in front of a barn, a row of children's vehicles on the left"
  caption="Pitch on the farm"
>}}

At least there is a small restaurant run by the owner right on the farm, and there we get really tasty Asian food. Freshly made? Yes. Tasty? Absolutely. Only our stomachs see it differently the next morning, and actually quite clearly all through the night. You cannot win them all.

## Mering and Schwabmünchen

At the weekend we can cover some distance again. First stop is [Mering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mering), part of our old home. Here we meet our friend and Lou's breeder, and for Lou there is a very special reunion: she meets her sister from the same litter.

With a very full stomach we continue to [Schwabmünchen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwabm%C3%BCnchen), another part of our old home. There we park rather cheekily, but without blocking anyone, right on the car park of the Luitpoldpark. Sometimes you simply have to know where to find a spot for Mammothon.
