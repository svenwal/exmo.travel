## Back to Civilisation

After a few nights of absolute solitude up in the mountains, it's time to head back to civilisation. There are still a few tight hairpin bends to negotiate, plus a drive through a village with streets that are just a little too narrow for the Mammothon. But then things get surprisingly easy: wide, paved roads take us all the way to [Corinth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corinth). Almost too comfortable!

## Corinth

Once again, we find a spot in the middle of an [olive grove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive). It comes with toilets, showers and all those little luxuries you really appreciate after a few days off the beaten track. And we can finally empty our wastewater tanks, too. It's funny how much you learn to appreciate the simple things on the road.

Behind the trees a mountain rises, a fortress on its summit: [Acrocorinth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acrocorinth). We'll get to that later, it deserves a post of its own.

## The Corinth Canal

A visit to Corinth wouldn't be complete without seeing the famous [Corinth Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corinth_Canal). So we hop on a little boat and cruise along it in both directions. The canal is an impressive feat of engineering from the 19th century: it cuts through the isthmus between the Peloponnese and the mainland, and the rock walls rise steeply on both sides. On a boat in between, you feel pretty small.
