## Two stops along the way

Two shorter stops on the way to Madrid. In Losar de la Vera we land entirely unplanned in the middle of the carnival. The whole village is out: music, costumes, atmosphere everywhere. What a coincidence.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-madrid/losar-talavera-01.jpeg"

At Talavera de la Reina we restock our supplies. We also explore the impressive bridge and the town on foot - a genuinely lovely stopover.

## Across the city centre

Before the march itself we take a 15-kilometre walk right across the centre of [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid).

We start at the Matadero Madrid - a former cattle market and slaughterhouse with a slightly grim past, today a centre for art and culture.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-madrid/madrid-stadtwanderung-01.jpeg"
  alt="Matadero Madrid, a former slaughterhouse"
  caption="Starting at the Matadero"
>}}

From there we follow the Madrid Río for a long stretch, with its green spaces, bridges and paths right along the river.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-madrid/madrid-stadtwanderung-02.jpeg"
  alt="Madrid Río with green spaces and bridges"
  caption="Along the Madrid Río"
>}}

After a short detour to check in for the Mammutmarsch, the route climbs to the Palacio Real. There we walk straight into a state visit.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-madrid/madrid-stadtwanderung-04.jpeg"
  alt="Palacio Real de Madrid"
  caption="Palacio Real - complete with state visit"
>}}

## Mammutmarsch

On the Mammutmarsch you can walk 30, 42, 50, 75 or even 100 kilometres, depending on how you feel, in various cities.

We opted for the "small" 30-kilometre loop, because Lou was coming along. Just to be safe, the handcart came too, so she could rest.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-madrid/madrid-01.jpeg"
  alt="Out on the Mammutmarsch in Madrid"
  caption="Out on the Mammutmarsch"
>}}

But Lou? Simply saw it through. Thirty kilometres, shortly before her 16th birthday. An absolute machine.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-madrid/madrid-02.jpeg"
  alt="Route around the Casa de Campo"
  caption="Around the Casa de Campo"

The route runs in perfect weather all around the beautiful Casa de Campo - an enormous park right beside the city.

## Castillo de Oreja

Out of the big city and straight into its complete opposite: we park up for a few days at Oreja - a ghost town with a very old castle dating from the Moorish period.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-madrid/castillo-de-oreja-01.jpeg"
  alt="Castle ruins of Castillo de Oreja"
  caption="Castillo de Oreja"
>}}

The drive in was tricky in places, but in the end we are rewarded with views like these.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-02-madrid/castillo-de-oreja-02.jpeg"
  alt="View from the castle at Oreja"
  caption="Worth a tricky approach"
>}}

So we stay several nights, enjoying the absolute quiet and solitude - and the by now pleasant daytime temperatures.
