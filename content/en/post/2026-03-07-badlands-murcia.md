## Marchal

We reach the badlands near Marchal - a raw, fascinating landscape.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-badlands-murcia/marchal-01.jpeg"
  alt="Badlands landscape near Marchal"
  caption="Badlands near Marchal"
>}}

The small municipal pitch is basic but perfectly located: within walking distance of the village and right on the edge of the landscape.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-badlands-murcia/marchal-02.jpeg"
  alt="Eroded rock formations near Marchal"
  caption="Raw and fascinating"
>}}

Saskia explores the well-known caves with Lou and does a long loop up onto the plateau. The landscape here really is something quite special.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-badlands-murcia/marchal-04.jpeg"
  alt="Plateau above the badlands near Marchal"
  caption="Up on the plateau"
>}}

At night, though, comes the disillusionment: a nearby factory apparently has a faulty alarm system - and it goes off every few minutes. Who needs sleep.

## Alhama de Murcia

We head to another of those special places where you end up entirely alone. We drive up to a viewpoint in the badlands near Alhama de Murcia.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-badlands-murcia/alhama-de-murcia-01.jpeg"
  alt="Viewpoint in the badlands near Alhama de Murcia"
  caption="The viewpoint"
>}}

During the day a few people come by now and then to enjoy the view. At night the place belongs entirely to us.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-badlands-murcia/alhama-de-murcia-02.jpeg"
  alt="Badlands near Alhama de Murcia"
  caption="At night this place is ours"
>}}

That may also have something to do with the several signs warning against driving up here in a camper. And yes - some of the washouts along the route are genuinely serious.

{{< figure
  src="/images/blog/2026-03-badlands-murcia/alhama-de-murcia-04.jpeg"
  alt="Washed-out track near Alhama de Murcia"
  caption="The signs are not entirely wrong"
>}}

But places exactly like this are what make this journey so special.
